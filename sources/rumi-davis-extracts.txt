This is Love: to fly heavenward, To rend, every instant, a hundred veils.

O, soul, if thou, too, wouldst be free, Then love the Love that shuts thee in.

Love is the astrolabe of God's mysteries.

However much we describe and explain Love, When we fall in love we are ashamed of our words.

Explanation by the tongue makes most things clear, But Love unexplained is better.

Pain is a treasure, for it contains mercies;

When the Love of God arises in thy heart, Without doubt God also feels love for thee.

Keep God in remembrance till self is forgotten, That thou may be lost in the Called, without distraction of caller and call.

A burning heart is what I want; consort with burning!

Kindle in thy heart the flame of Love, And burn up utterly thoughts and fine expressions.

The mosque that is built in the hearts of the saints Is the place of worship of all, for God dwells there.

No sickness worse than fancying thyself perfect Can infect thy soul, O arrogant misguided one!

He who extracts the rose from the thorn Can also turn this winter into spring.

The Love of the soul is for wisdom and knowledge, That of the body for houses, gardens, and vine-yards;

Renounce these affections for outward forms, Love depends not on outward form or face.

Earthly forms are only shadows of the Sun of Truth--a cradle for babes, but too small to hold those who have grown to spiritual manhood.

Trust in God, yet tie the camel's leg.

Be silent and free from the pain of speech: do not slumber, Since thou hast taken refuge with so loving a Friend.

A heavy slumber fell upon thee from the circling spheres: Alas, for this life so light, beware of this slumber so heavy!

Happy the moment when we are seated in the Palace, thou and I, With two forms and with two figures but with one soul, thou and I.

Our worldly goods rob us of our heavenly goods, Our body rends the garment of our soul.

How long wilt thou dwell on words and superficialities?

Conceive the Soul as a fountain, and these created things as rivers: While the Fountain flows, the rivers run from it.

Love exalts our earthly bodies to heaven, And makes the very hills to dance with joy!

Whatever withdraws thine eyes from God is an idol.

The BELOVED is all in all, the lover only veils Him; The BELOVED is all that lives, the lover a dead thing.

Look not in the world for bliss and fortune, since thou wilt not find them;

For God had set His likeness on all the things that were.
