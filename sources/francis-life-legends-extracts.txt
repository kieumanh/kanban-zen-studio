Let us now begin to serve the Lord our God, for up to this time we have made but little progress.

May the Lord grant you His peace.

Be Thou praised, my Lord, for death our sister, from which no living man can escape,

Blessed are they who, at the hour of death, are found conformed to Thy holy will, for they will not be overtaken by the second death.

May all creatures praise and bless God, obey Him and serve Him with great humility!

we must not let this time, which is consecrated to God, pass without praising His holy name, and confessing our own misery.

the most considerable is, that of conquering one's self, and of suffering pain and opprobrium for the love of God, in order to respond to the love He has for us.

if we bear all this ill usage with joy, with the thought that we ought to participate in the sufferings of our Blessed Saviour Jesus Christ, note this, and note it carefully, that this is, for a Friar Minor, the subject of a true and perfect joy.
